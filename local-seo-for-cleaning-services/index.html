Questions, answered

How does Eagle Eye help a cleaning company rank?

It scans the whole service area on a grid and shows, point by point, where you win the booking for “house cleaning near me” and where a rival quietly takes it — a cleaning company never sees a walk-in.

Can I shape the scan to the exact area I cover?

Yes. Set a radius and draw the scan as a square, circle, or an area shaped by hand — points outside it aren’t searched or charged.

Why isn’t one map pin enough?

You can rank first at your address and still be invisible in the suburbs you serve. A grid shows every one.

Can I see which services win the suburbs I miss?

Eagle Eye draws a competitor map for every rival that outranks you — their rank at every point of the same grid — so you see exactly which competitor wins each block, not just that you place #5.

Is there a free scan to start?

A new organisation starts with 10 free scans, and your first scan of your own website is free at any size up to 21 × 21 — no card needed.
