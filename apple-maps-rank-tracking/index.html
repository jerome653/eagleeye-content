Track Apple Maps the same way you track Google

Apple Maps sends real customers to local businesses, and almost no rank tool watches it. Eagle Eye does: the same geo-grid, the same numbers, at the same price as Google Maps — one credit per point, up to 441 points in a 21 × 21 scan.

One scan reads one map

Each scan searches a single map — Google Maps or Apple Maps — so your credits and your results stay clean and comparable. Run a business on both and you get two maps side by side: where it ranks on Google, where it ranks on Apple, and where the two disagree. For many local businesses the Apple picture is the one nobody has ever measured.

Questions, answered

Can you track Apple Maps rankings?

Yes. Eagle Eye runs the same geo-grid on Apple Maps as on Google — the same numbers, the same price — so you can measure a surface almost no other rank tool watches.

Is Apple Maps tracking the same price as Google?

Identical: one credit per grid point, whether the scan reads Apple Maps or Google Maps.

What’s different about an Apple Maps scan?

Everything is the same except one thing: Apple Maps doesn’t publish review counts, so none are shown for an Apple scan. Rank, LSOV, ARP, ATRP, competitor maps and every export are all there.

Can I compare Google and Apple side by side?

Yes. Each scan reads one map, so run a business on both and you get two maps side by side — where it ranks on Google, where it ranks on Apple, and where the two disagree.

How big can an Apple Maps scan be?

A grid runs from 9 × 9 (81 points) up to 21 × 21 (441 points), always an odd size so the business sits on the exact centre point. Set a radius from 0.1 mile and shape it as a square, a circle, or an area you draw.
