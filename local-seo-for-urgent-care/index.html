Be the clinic “near me now” finds first

A worried patient searching “urgent care near me” or “walk in clinic open now” goes to the nearest one the map shows — a block too far down the pack and they’re at the clinic across the road instead. A single rank can’t warn you when you slip. Eagle Eye shows where you rank across the catchment on both Google and Apple Maps, and alerts you the instant you fall off the map on a block, before the walk-ins do.

Hold the top when it counts

  • Catchment rank — a grid over the area patients search from, the clinic on the centre point.
  • Drop-out alerts — hear it the moment you fall out of the top 20 on a block.
  • Both maps — patients search on whichever phone is in their hand, Google or Apple.
  • LSOV — the share of the catchment that sees the clinic in the top 3 when it’s needed.
