Asked before you sign up

Anything else is in the user guide inside the app.

How is a rank measured?

From each point of the grid, a browser searches Google Maps for your keyword and reads the top 20 listings. Your rank at that point is where your business appears in them.

Why do grid sizes go up in odd numbers?

So the grid has a centre point on the business and the same number of points on every side.

What does a scan cost?

One credit per point searched: a 9 × 9 grid is 81 credits. A new organisation starts with 10 free scans, and your first scan of the website you sign up with is free at any size.

Can I compare two scans of the same business?

Yes. A campaign does it on every run, and Run again repeats any scan with the same settings, so the trend builds up on its own.

Does it search Apple Maps?

Yes. Each scan reads one map, Google Maps or Apple Maps, at the same price: one credit a point. Run a business on both to compare them. Apple Maps shows no review counts, so none are shown for an Apple scan.

Do unused credits expire?

No. Credits stay in your organisation's balance until you spend them, whichever plan you are on.

Can I schedule scans?

Yes. Any scan can run once, daily, weekly or monthly, and every run is kept so you can see the trend.

Is my first scan free?

Yes. When you sign up with your website, your first scan of that business is free at any grid size up to 21 × 21. Each website gets it once. A new organisation also starts with 10 free scans.

What if a scheduled scan runs out of credits?

It still runs, so no point in your history is missed, but the result stays locked until you top up or subscribe. Up to 5 scans can wait locked at once, for 30 days. The Spending page shows who spent the last credits.

What can I download?

A scan's listings as a CSV, reports as PDF or CSV, and a time-lapse GIF of every run of a scan.

Three numbers, every scan

The whole grid rolls up into the figures a client actually remembers — tracked every run so the trend is the proof.

LSOVLocal Share of Voice

How much of the whole grid finds the business near the top — one number for the entire map.

ARPAverage Rank, found

The mean position across only the points that return the business at all.

ATRPAverage Total Rank

Questions, answered

What is geo-grid rank tracking?

Instead of one rank for a keyword, Eagle Eye lays a grid of points over a business’s neighbourhood and runs a real Google Maps or Apple Maps search from each one. You see your rank street by street, because local results change with every block the searcher stands on.

What grid sizes can I run?

From 9 × 9 up to 21 × 21 points. Sizes are always odd so the business sits on the exact centre point, with the same number of points on every side. A 21 × 21 grid is 441 searches in one sweep.

Can I limit the scan to an area?

Yes. Set a radius from 0.1 mile and up, in miles or kilometres, and shape the scan as a square, a circle, or an area you draw. Points outside the shape are not searched and not charged.

What alerts can I set?

Eagle Eye flags a fall in LSOV, a worsening rank, grid points dropping out of the top 20, or a rival taking the top 3 — as soon as a scan finds it, before the client does.

What can I export?

A scan’s listings as CSV, your reports as PDF or CSV, and a time-lapse GIF of every run of a scan. Reports carry your own logo, colours and footer.

Who can I add to an account?

Each organisation has owners, admins and members, and API keys so you can start scans and read results from your own code. Agencies keep one organisation per client, with a single credit balance paying for all.
