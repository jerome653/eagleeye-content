Your Business Profile doesn’t rank in one place

A Google Business Profile shows up near the top for some searchers and nowhere for others, because Google Maps reorders the local results by where the searcher is standing. A single “we rank #3” hides every block where the profile is invisible. Eagle Eye measures where a Business Profile actually appears across a whole area — a grid of up to 441 points, a real Google Maps search from each one, the rank of the listing street by street. It reads the rank; it doesn’t touch the profile.

What one scan reads

  • Rank at every grid point on a real Google Maps search, 9 × 9 up to 21 × 21.

Questions, answered

How do I track where my Google Business Profile ranks?

Eagle Eye measures where a Business Profile actually appears across a whole area — a grid of up to 441 points, a real Google Maps search from each, the rank of the listing street by street. It reads the rank; it doesn’t touch the profile.

Does it change or manage my profile?

No. Eagle Eye only reads where the profile ranks on the map — it never edits or posts to the listing.

Can I see which rival profiles outrank mine?

Eagle Eye draws a competitor map for every rival that outranks you — their rank at every point of the same grid — so you see exactly which competitor wins each block, not just that you place #5.

Can I track the profile on Apple Maps too?

Yes. Eagle Eye runs the same geo-grid on Google Maps and on Apple Maps at the same price. Each scan reads one map, so run both and you get the two side by side — including the Apple picture most tools never measure.
