Questions, answered

How does Eagle Eye help a car dealership rank locally?

A dealership pulls buyers from a wide catchment, and the map ranks it differently from every town in that reach. Eagle Eye lays a wide grid of up to 441 points over the whole catchment and shows where you win and where a rival lot does.

Can I cover the full distance buyers travel?

Yes — set a 21 × 21 grid to the real catchment radius buyers come from, so no town on the edge of your reach is missed.

Can I see which dealerships outrank me in outlying towns?

Eagle Eye draws a competitor map for every rival that outranks you — their rank at every point of the same grid — so you see exactly which competitor wins each block, not just that you place #5.

Does it track both Google and Apple Maps?

Yes. Eagle Eye runs the same geo-grid on Google Maps and on Apple Maps at the same price. Each scan reads one map, so run both and you get the two side by side — including the Apple picture most tools never measure.

Can I track a group of lots?

Yes — run each lot on its own grid and compare them on the same three numbers.
