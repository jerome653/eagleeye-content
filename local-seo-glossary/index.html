The words behind the map

Local map rank tracking has its own vocabulary. Here it is in plain language — every term as Eagle Eye uses it.

Geo-grid rank tracking

Instead of one rank for a keyword, a grid of points is laid over a business and a real map search is run from each one. The result is the rank street by street, because local results change with every block the searcher stands on.

Grid / grid point

The lattice of search locations in a scan, from 9 × 9 (81 points) to 21 × 21 (441). Each point is one real map search, and one credit.

LSOV — Local Share of Voice

The share of a scan’s grid points where the business ranks in the top 3. One number for how much of the neighbourhood sees it near the top. More on the numbers.

ARP — Average Rank where Present

The average position across only the points where the business appears in the top 20 — how high it ranks where it ranks at all.

ATRP — Average Total Rank

The same average, but every point where the business is missing counts as 21, so the blank parts of the map are measured instead of ignored.

Local pack

The block of (usually three) map results shown above the regular links for a local search. Appearing in it is what LSOV measures.

Competitor map

A geo-grid map of a rival’s rank at every point of the same scan — where they beat you and where they don’t.

Service-area business (SAB)

A business that serves customers across an area rather than at a storefront — a plumber, an HVAC company, a mobile service. Geo-grid tracking matters most here, because a single pin hides the whole area. More on home services.

Credit

The unit a scan spends: one credit per grid point searched. An 81-point scan costs 81 credits; a 441-point scan costs 441. Credits never expire.

Time-lapse

Every run of a scan stitched into a short GIF, so a month of movement reads at a glance.
