Questions, answered

How does Eagle Eye help a real estate agent rank locally?

It shows which neighbourhoods — farms — put you on top of the map and which belong to a rival working the same streets, so you win the listing calls instead of losing them unseen.

Can I track several farms separately?

Yes. Run each farm on its own grid, each with its own LSOV, ARP and ATRP, and compare them on the same numbers.

Can I hand a seller a branded map?

Yes — export a white-label PDF with your own logo and colours, a clean map a seller can read at a glance.

Can I see which agents own the blocks I want?

Eagle Eye draws a competitor map for every rival that outranks you — their rank at every point of the same grid — so you see exactly which competitor wins each block, not just that you place #5.

What does it cost to start?

A new organisation starts with 10 free scans, and your first scan of your own website is free at any size up to 21 × 21 — no card needed.
