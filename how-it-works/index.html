Rankings are abstract; being found is not. Questions, answered

How do I prove local SEO to a client with Eagle Eye?

Run a scan to find the gap (where they’re invisible on the map), put the same scan on a schedule to prove the lift (LSOV climbing run over run), then export a branded report with your name on it. The map makes abstract rankings something a client can see.

How long does a scan take to set up?

Pick a point on the map, choose a grid size and radius, set the keyword, and run. The business sits on the centre point automatically; a scan reads one map (Google or Apple) so results stay clean.

How often should I re-run a scan?

Put it on a schedule — daily, weekly or monthly — and Eagle Eye keeps every run so the trend builds itself. Most agencies run a monthly scan per client and let alerts flag anything between runs.

What do I hand the client at the end?

A branded PDF or CSV carrying your logo, colours and footer: the geo-grid map, the three numbers, the competitor picture, and a time-lapse GIF of the movement over time.

Can I try it on a client before I pay?

A new organisation starts with 10 free scans, and your first scan of your own website is free at any size up to 21 × 21 — no card needed.
