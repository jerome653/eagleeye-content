The data an ordinary rank checker can’t show

Most rank tools give you a position and stop. A geo-grid scan records the ground-level map of a neighbourhood: every rival’s rank at every point, who was open when each point was searched, review counts moving between scans, and the full top 20 at each point with rating, category and address — up to 8,820 listings from one 21 × 21 scan. Three numbers a client remembers and a trend that proves your work paid off. Every scan rolls the whole grid into them — no spreadsheet, no hand-waving.

Questions, answered

What data does a geo-grid scan capture that a rank checker doesn’t?

The ground-level map of a neighbourhood: every rival’s rank at every point, who was open when each point was searched, review counts moving between scans, and the full top 20 at each point with rating, category and address — up to 8,820 listings from one 21 × 21 scan.

Can I see every competitor, not just my own rank?

Eagle Eye draws a competitor map for every rival that outranks you — their rank at every point of the same grid — so you see exactly which competitor wins each block, not just that you place #5.

Does it record changes over time?

Yes. Every run of a scan is kept, so review moves and rank shifts between scans are recorded and can be replayed as a time-lapse.

How many listings can one scan return?

A 21 × 21 scan reads 441 points, and each point records the full top 20 — up to 8,820 listings in a single sweep.

What does a scan cost?

Every plan is a bucket of credits and a scan spends one credit per grid point: 81 credits for a 9 × 9 scan, up to 441 for a 21 × 21. Points you switch off or leave outside your shape cost nothing, and credits you buy never expire.
