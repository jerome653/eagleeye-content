The details

What you can set, what you get back, and what it costs.

What it searches

Maps
Google Maps and Apple Maps, each its own scan at the same price.
At every point
The top 20 listings for your keyword, as someone standing there would see them.
Grid size
Always odd, so the business sits on the centre point.9×911×1113×1315×1517×1719×1921×21
Radius
From 0.1 mile, in miles or kilometres, measured from the centre to the edge.
Shape
A square, a circle, or an area you draw. Points outside it are not searched or charged.

What you get back

Three numbers
LSOVshare of points in the top 3ARPaverage rank where foundATRPaverage, a missed point counted as 21
The map
Every point coloured by your rank. Click one to see the 20 listings there and who sits above you.
Competitors
Every business that ranks above you, how often, how high, and where on the map.
The trend
Each business keeps every run, with LSOV over time, filtered by keyword and grid size.
Downloads
Listings, reports and every run as a time-lapse.CSVPDFGIF

What keeps it running

Run again
Any scan, with the same settings, in one click.
Schedules
Once, daily, weekdays, chosen days, monthly or every N days, in your own time zone.
Campaigns
Many locations and keywords on one schedule.
Alerts
When LSOV falls, rank worsens, points drop out of the top 20, or a rival takes the top 3.

Team and cost

Your team
Owners, admins and members in each organisation, and API keys for your own code.
Per scan
1 credit per point searched.9×9 = 8111×11 = 12121×21 = 441
Credits
Credits you buy never expire. Reward and referral credits last 90 days.

10 free scans for a new organisation, and your own website's first scan free.
