Questions, answered

What does one geo-grid scan give you?

A rank at every grid point, your LSOV, ARP and ATRP, a competitor map for every rival that outranks you, and every export — on Google Maps or Apple Maps.

What can I automate?

Schedules (daily, weekly or monthly), campaigns that group related scans, and alerts when LSOV falls, a rank worsens, points drop out of the top 20, or a rival takes the top 3.

What can I export?

Branded PDF and CSV reports with your own logo, colours and footer, plus a time-lapse GIF stitched from every run of a scan.

Is there an API and team access?

Yes — API keys to start scans and read results from your own code, and team seats with owners, admins and members so a whole team works from the same scans.

How much does it cost to run?

Every plan is a bucket of credits and a scan spends one credit per grid point: 81 credits for a 9 × 9 scan, up to 441 for a 21 × 21. Points you switch off or leave outside your shape cost nothing, and credits you buy never expire.
