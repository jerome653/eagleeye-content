Every “shoe store near me” or “gift shop open now” that finds a competitor first is a sale that walked past your door. A one-number rank tool can’t tell you where that’s happening — retail is won block by block, and the map pack reorders with every corner a shopper stands on. Eagle Eye shows the exact streets where the store wins foot traffic and the ones handing it to the shop around the corner, on both Google and Apple Maps — the surface iPhone shoppers use and most stores never check.

Questions, answered

How does Eagle Eye help a retail store rank locally?

It maps the store’s rank block by block for “near me” and “open now” searches, on both Google and Apple Maps, so you see the exact streets you win walk-ins and the ones handing them to a shop nearby.

Why track Apple Maps for a store?

iPhone shoppers navigate on Apple Maps and most stores never check it. Eagle Eye tracks it at the same price as Google, so you win shoppers a Google-only tool never sees.

Can I see which shops are catching my “near me” searches?

Eagle Eye draws a competitor map for every rival that outranks you — their rank at every point of the same grid — so you see exactly which competitor wins each block, not just that you place #5.

Can I try it on my store first?

A new organisation starts with 10 free scans, and your first scan of your own website is free at any size up to 21 × 21 — no card needed.
