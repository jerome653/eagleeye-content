Questions, answered

How does Eagle Eye help a self-storage facility rank locally?

It runs a real “storage units near me” map search from a grid across the catchment, so you see where the facility ranks in the top 3 and which competing facility is being found instead.

Can I track several facilities?

Yes — each site runs on its own grid with its own LSOV, so you can compare facilities on the same numbers from one account.

Can I see which facility outranks me?

Eagle Eye draws a competitor map for every rival that outranks you — their rank at every point of the same grid — so you see exactly which competitor wins each block, not just that you place #5.

What do LSOV, ARP and ATRP mean?

Every scan folds the grid into three figures: LSOV (the share of points where you rank in the top 3), ARP (your average rank where you appear in the top 20) and ATRP (the same average, counting every missing point as 21 so the blank parts of the map are measured too).

What does a scan cost?

Every plan is a bucket of credits and a scan spends one credit per grid point: 81 credits for a 9 × 9 scan, up to 441 for a 21 × 21. Points you switch off or leave outside your shape cost nothing, and credits you buy never expire.
