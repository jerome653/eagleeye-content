Questions, answered

What is Local Share of Voice (LSOV)?

LSOV is the share of a scan’s grid points where a business ranks in the top 3 — one number for how much of the neighbourhood sees it near the top of the map, and the one line to open a report with.

Why is LSOV the number to report?

It folds a whole grid of up to 441 points into a single percentage anyone can follow, it’s built on the top 3 (the only positions that get clicks and calls), and it moves run over run so the trend is obvious.

How is LSOV different from ARP and ATRP?

LSOV is reach — the share of the area in the top 3. ARP is height — your average rank where you appear. ATRP is both, counting missing points as 21.

Does LSOV update automatically?

Yes — put the scan on a schedule and LSOV builds itself, run over run.
