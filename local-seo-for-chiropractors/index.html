Capture the patients searching in pain

Someone with a bad back picks a clinic near them off the map and books — usually before they scroll past the top three. Every “chiropractor near me” that ranks a competing clinic first is a new patient you lost without knowing. A one-number rank tool can’t find those gaps. Eagle Eye shows where your practice tops the catchment and, just as important, every block where it’s missing from the map entirely.

Find the patients you’re missing

  • Catchment rank — a grid over the area patients search from, the clinic on the centre point.
  • ATRP — a single number for the blocks where you don’t appear at all, turned into something you can watch move.
  • A competitor map per rival — the clinics taking the searches on the streets you don’t cover.
  • Scheduled scans — the same grid run monthly, so the catchment’s trend builds itself.
