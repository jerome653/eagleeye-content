Local map rank tracking, point by point

A rank for “pizza near me” is only true from one spot: wherever you checked it. On Google Maps and Apple Maps, the top three shift block by block, so one number won’t hold up in front of a client. Eagle Eye is a geo-grid rank tracker. It lays up to 441 points over a business’s neighborhood and runs a real map search from each one. You see the rank street by street: where it wins, where it falls off the map, and which rival is taking those customers.

Every scan rolls the grid into three numbers — LSOV, ARP, and ATRP — maps every competitor that outranks your client, and saves each run so the trend builds itself. Built for agencies and marketers who have to prove local SEO to the people paying for it.

Asked before you sign up

Anything else is in the user guide inside the app.

How is a rank measured?

From each point of the grid, a browser searches Google Maps for your keyword and reads the top 20 listings. Your rank at that point is where your business appears in them.

Why do grid sizes go up in odd numbers?

So the grid has a center point on the business and the same number of points on every side.

What does a scan cost?

One credit per point searched: a 9 × 9 grid is 81 credits. A new organization starts with 10 free scans, and your first scan of the website you sign up with is free at any size.

Can I compare two scans of the same business?

Yes. A campaign does it on every run, and Run again repeats any scan with the same settings, so the trend builds up on its own.

Does it search Apple Maps?

Yes. Each scan reads one map, Google Maps or Apple Maps, at the same price: one credit a point. Run a business on both to compare them. Apple Maps shows no review counts, so none are shown for an Apple scan.

Do unused credits expire?

No. Credits stay in your organization's balance until you spend them, whichever plan you are on.

Can I schedule scans?

Yes. Any scan can run once, daily, weekly or monthly, and every run is kept so you can see the trend.

Is my first scan free?

Yes. When you sign up with your website, your first scan of that business is free at any grid size up to 21 × 21. Each website gets it once. A new organization also starts with 10 free scans.

What if a scheduled scan runs out of credits?

It still runs, so no point in your history is missed, but the result stays locked until you top up or subscribe. Up to 5 scans can wait locked at once, for 30 days. The Spending page shows who spent the last credits.

Does Eagle Eye use AI to answer my questions?

No. Ask the map matches your question against the kinds it understands and answers from the scan's own points, so the same question always gets the same answer and nothing is sent to an AI service. If you use an AI assistant of your own, an API key lets it read your scans.

Can results go straight into our own tools?

Yes. Set a webhook in Settings and each finished scan and scheduled report is posted to your https address, signed so you can check it came from Eagle Eye. API keys let your own code start scans and read results.

What can I download?

A scan's listings as a CSV, reports as PDF or CSV, and a time-lapse GIF of every run of a scan.
