How big should your grid be?

A geo-grid scan is as wide and as fine as you set it. The grid runs from 9 × 9 (81 points) up to 21 × 21 (441 points), always an odd number so the business sits dead centre with the same reach on every side. And because every point is one credit, the size you pick is also what the scan costs — so a grid is a choice between how much area you cover and how much you spend.

Setting the scan

  • Grid: 9 × 9 (81 points) to 21 × 21 (441) — wider covers more area, finer resolves more detail.
  • Radius: from 0.1 mile and up, in miles or kilometres, measured out from the centre point.
  • Shape: a square, a circle, or an area you draw — points outside it aren’t searched and aren’t charged.
  • Cost: one credit per point searched — 81 at 9 × 9, 441 at 21 × 21 — and the credits you buy never expire.

Not sure what the points add up to? LSOV, ARP and ATRP turn any grid size into the same three numbers. Questions, answered

What grid sizes can I run?

A grid runs from 9 × 9 (81 points) up to 21 × 21 (441 points), always an odd size so the business sits on the exact centre point. Set a radius from 0.1 mile and shape it as a square, a circle, or an area you draw.

How many points is each grid size?

A 9 × 9 grid is 81 points; a 21 × 21 grid is 441. The size is always odd so the business sits on the exact centre point.

How do I set the radius and shape?

Set a radius from 0.1 mile and up, in miles or kilometres, and shape the scan as a square, a circle, or an area you draw. Points outside the shape aren’t searched or charged.

How does grid size affect cost?

You pay one credit per point, so a wider or finer grid costs more — 81 credits at 9 × 9, 441 at 21 × 21. Credits never expire.

What size should I pick?

Wider covers more area; finer resolves more detail. Try a size on your own business free — your first own-site scan is free at any size up to 21 × 21.
