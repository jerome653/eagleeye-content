Where a business ranks on Google Maps, point by point

On Google Maps the three results in the local pack change with every block the searcher is standing on. One rank for “coffee near me” is true at one spot and nowhere else. Eagle Eye lays a grid of up to 441 points over a business and runs a real Google Maps search from every one, so you get the rank street by street — where it wins the pack, where it slips, and which rival is sitting in its place.

What one Google Maps scan gives you

  • Rank at every grid point, 9 × 9 up to 21 × 21, on a real Google Maps search.
  • LSOV, ARP and ATRP — the three numbers that turn the grid into an answer a client remembers.
  • A competitor map for every rival that outranks you, point by point.
  • Scheduled scans, branded reports, time-lapse and alerts — the full toolkit, on Google.

Three numbers, every scan

The whole grid rolls up into the figures a client actually remembers — tracked every run so the trend is the proof.

LSOVLocal Share of Voice

How much of the whole grid finds the business near the top — one number for the entire map.

ARPAverage Rank, found

The mean position across only the points that return the business at all.

ATRPAverage Total Rank

The same average, counting every point that missed the business entirely — the honest one.

Questions, answered

How do I track Google Maps rankings across a whole area?

Eagle Eye lays a grid of up to 441 points over a business and runs a real Google Maps search from every one, so you see the rank street by street — where it wins the local pack, where it slips, and which rival sits in its place.

Does it track the Google local 3-pack?

Yes. Every grid point records whether the business makes the top 3 there, so LSOV tells you the share of the area that sees it in the pack.

Can I see competitors on Google Maps?

Eagle Eye draws a competitor map for every rival that outranks you — their rank at every point of the same grid — so you see exactly which competitor wins each block, not just that you place #5.

Can I track Apple Maps too?

Yes. Eagle Eye runs the same geo-grid on Google Maps and on Apple Maps at the same price. Each scan reads one map, so run both and you get the two side by side — including the Apple picture most tools never measure.

What does a Google Maps scan cost?

Every plan is a bucket of credits and a scan spends one credit per grid point: 81 credits for a 9 × 9 scan, up to 441 for a 21 × 21. Points you switch off or leave outside your shape cost nothing, and credits you buy never expire.
