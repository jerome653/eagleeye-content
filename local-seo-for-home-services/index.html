Questions, answered

How do I track local SEO for a service-area business?

A plumber, HVAC company or contractor has no storefront — just a service area. Eagle Eye scans that whole area on a grid and shows, point by point, where the business ranks and where a competitor owns the call.

Why isn’t a single map pin enough?

A service-area business can rank #1 at its base and vanish three suburbs out. A single pin hides that; a grid shows both the strong blocks and the blank ones.

Can I get alerted when I drop out of the pack?

Yes — set alerts and hear the moment you fall out of the top 20 or a rival takes the top 3 in a key area.

Can I shape the scan to my exact service area?

Yes. Set a radius from 0.1 mile and draw the scan as a square, circle, or an area you shape by hand — points outside it aren’t searched or charged.

What does it cost?

Every plan is a bucket of credits and a scan spends one credit per grid point: 81 credits for a 9 × 9 scan, up to 441 for a 21 × 21. Points you switch off or leave outside your shape cost nothing, and credits you buy never expire.
