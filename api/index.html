Rank data from your own code

Everything Eagle Eye does in the app, it can do from your own code. Create API keys, start a scan, and read the results — the points, the ranks, the competitor data — straight into your own dashboards, client portals and workflows.

What the API is for

  • Start scans programmatically — kick off a geo-grid scan from your own system.
  • Read the results — pull LSOV, ARP, ATRP and the per-point ranks for a scan.
  • Build it into your stack — feed the numbers into the dashboards and reports your clients already see.
  • Your own keys — issued per organisation, under your team’s control.

Agencies use the API to put live local-rank data into client portals — see the agency workflow. See the plans.

Three numbers, every scan

The whole grid rolls up into the figures a client actually remembers — tracked every run so the trend is the proof.

LSOVLocal Share of Voice

How much of the whole grid finds the business near the top — one number for the entire map.

ARPAverage Rank, found

The mean position across only the points that return the business at all.

ATRPAverage Total Rank

The same average, counting every point that missed the business entirely — the honest one.

Questions, answered

Does Eagle Eye have an API?

Yes — create API keys, start a scan, and read the results (the points, the ranks, the competitor data) straight into your own dashboards, portals and workflows.

What can I do with the API?

Start geo-grid scans programmatically and pull LSOV, ARP, ATRP and the per-point ranks for a scan into your own stack.

Who controls the API keys?

Keys are issued per organisation, under your team’s control.

Why do agencies use the API?

To put live local-rank data into the client portals and dashboards their clients already see.

What does it cost?

Every plan is a bucket of credits and a scan spends one credit per grid point: 81 credits for a 9 × 9 scan, up to 441 for a 21 × 21. Points you switch off or leave outside your shape cost nothing, and credits you buy never expire.
